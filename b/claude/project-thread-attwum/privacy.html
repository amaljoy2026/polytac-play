PolyTac privacy policy

Fenix-AI · last updated 8 November 2026

PolyTac is a single-player game. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the game stores

Your current run and your settings (sound, music, hints) are saved on your own device so you can continue later. They never leave your device, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in or personal details.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No internet connection is used while you play.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

Bug reports

"Copy bug report" copies a description of your run (the game's build and a replay of your moves) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere: it is only shared if you choose to paste it into a message to us yourself.

Children

PolyTac is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: use the developer contact email on PolyTac's Google Play page.
